## Prompt

Create one production-ready transparent raster texture for a website gift voucher. The attached photograph is ONLY A STYLE REFERENCE for the morphology of real frost: intricate fine feathery ice ferns, layered branching crystalline needles, natural irregular growth, subtle translucent icy white and silver-blue highlights, macro photographic realism. Generate a wide horizontal texture with approximately 2.2:1 aspect ratio. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND, genuine alpha; no photographed background, no solid glass sheet, no scenery, no text, no logo, no ticket or lettering. Composition: organic uneven frost grows INWARD FROM BOTH SIDE EDGES AND CORNERS, scattered asymmetric patches with varied lengths, density and orientation. Stronger feathery growth in upper left corner, patchy needles lower left, fine crystalline ferns along the upper right and lower right edges. The outer 10-20% at BOTH left and right sides must contain visible delicate frost, not just the center. Keep most of the central 60% transparent and also leave irregular clear pockets around the edges. A few short wisps along the top and bottom; no uniform frame, no rectangle, no repeated or mirrored shapes, no vector line drawings, no geometric snowflake icons. Frost should look grown naturally on a cold winter window like the supplied reference, with rich microtexture and tapering fern branches. This asset will be overlaid on a very dark evergreen voucher, whose cream text panel will cover the center. Output only the transparent frost texture.
